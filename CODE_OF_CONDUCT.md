# HookGuard Community Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and skill levels.

## Expectations

- Discuss technical disagreements constructively, with evidence and curiosity.
- Give credit for others’ work and respect intellectual-property rights.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or intimidate other participants.
- Do not publish private information, credentials or unreported vulnerability details.
- Do not submit spam, manufactured issues, or low-value pull requests.

## Reporting

For community conduct problems, use a private channel to contact the repository maintainer where available. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue. The maintainer may remove abusive content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will handle reports as fairly and confidentially as possible.
